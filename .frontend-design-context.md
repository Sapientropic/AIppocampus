# Frontend Design Context

AIppocampus public web surfaces should feel calm, human, and source-grounded.
The product is a local-first continuity layer, not a generic AI dashboard or a
marketing funnel.

Use restrained typography, generous spacing, warm paper tones, deep ink blues,
and one strong image when the page needs emotional orientation. Avoid glossy AI
gradients, dashboard mosaics, exaggerated claims, and content that mirrors long
repository docs.

Canonical content remains in the repository docs and Wiki. Public web pages
should act as signposts: what AIppocampus is, why it matters, how to inspect it,
and what it cannot claim yet.
