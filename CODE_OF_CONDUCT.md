# Code Of Conduct

![Two people carrying a lantern toward a distant horizon](.github/assets/community/code-of-conduct-lantern.jpg)

AIppocampus is built around continuity, source, and care. The same posture
applies to how we work together.

## Our Standard

Be clear, patient, and specific. Assume good intent, but do not let harm hide
behind ambiguity.

Good participation includes:

- grounding claims in source, tests, or clearly marked uncertainty
- keeping private memory artifacts, credentials, local paths, and personal data
  out of public threads
- disagreeing with ideas without belittling people
- giving contributors enough context to repair a problem
- respecting that this project touches memory, identity, and trust

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, doxxing, credential exposure, and repeated bad-faith
misrepresentation of another contributor's work.

## Private Data Boundary

Do not post raw conversation exports, local registry data, secret-bearing logs,
private source refs, or screenshots that reveal personal information. If a bug
needs private evidence, describe the shape of the evidence and offer a sanitized
fixture or redacted trace instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, restrict participation,
or block contributors when needed to keep the project safe and workable.

When reporting a conduct concern, include links, screenshots, or short context
when safe to share. Do not include private memory artifacts just to prove a
point.

This code is intentionally small. The rule underneath it is simple: help people
keep the light on while we build tools that remember carefully.
