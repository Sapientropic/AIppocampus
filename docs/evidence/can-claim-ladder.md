# Can-Claim Ladder

Role: reader-facing positive evidence map.
Status: public interpretation layer for #1079.

This page answers a simple question before readers hit the caveats: what is
already proven, and where is the proof? It does not replace
[`current-claims.md`](current-claims.md), the stage readiness docs, or dated
verification ledgers. Those remain the source of truth for numbers,
supersession, and command evidence.

## Proven And Claimable Today

AIppocampus is a working local source-backed continuity layer, not only a
concept document.

- Proof slice: release-claimable local source-backed conversation memory in
  [`stage-0-5-readiness.md`](readiness/stage-0-5-readiness.md) and the
  install/update surfaces linked from [`README.md`](../../README.md).
- Can claim: local clean-source import/search/reopen paths and agent-readable
  continuity routes exist.
- Boundary: not hosted memory, not innate model memory, not universal recall
  quality.

AIppocampus can expose source-backed recall routes to agents.

- Proof slice: progressive recall navigation and `recall_context ->
  recall_deepen` evidence in
  [`current-claims.md`](current-claims.md) and
  [`benchmark-evidence-map.md`](benchmark-evidence-map.md).
- Can claim: deterministic public fixtures can return reopenable routes and
  source windows.
- Boundary: not broad live foreground usefulness or guaranteed hook delivery.

AIppocampus keeps source reopen above generated summaries.

- Proof slice: hard-negative source discipline, Track S robustness, multimodal
  evidence-pool, and source-review rows in
  [`current-claims.md`](current-claims.md).
- Can claim: selected public fixtures suppress wrong-source, stale-currentness,
  and unsupported-as-fact outputs.
- Boundary: not human-level semantic understanding or real high-risk answer
  certification.

AIppocampus has public-safe benchmark fixtures and external adapter paths.

- Proof slice: benchmark owner map in
  [`benchmark-evidence-map.md`](benchmark-evidence-map.md), AMemGym and
  STATE-Bench feasibility notes, LongMemEval retrieval slices, provider
  conformance fixtures, and the E2E50 public-safe behavior pack.
- Can claim: many failure modes are reproducible without private history.
- Boundary: adapter feasibility is not an official external score or product
  superiority claim.

## Field-Tested But Still Improving

Second-user and local dogfood reports show the product shape is useful, but
they are not broad statistical proof.

- Magic moments and field reports:
  [`magic-moments.md`](magic-moments.md) and
  [`community-field-reports.md`](community-field-reports.md).
- Fresh-thread and long-thread continuity:

  public and private-safe evidence.
- Hook foreground delivery: #1078 records the current gap between pull-style

cross-host quality, or broad private-history generality yet.

## Open Proof Walls

These are not failures by themselves; they are the next proof walls.

- SOTA, leaderboard superiority, or broad external benchmark dominance.
- Hosted/team memory service behavior.
- Innate model memory.
- Broad live user quality from deterministic fixtures.
- Medical, legal, therapy, compliance, or other high-risk answer quality.
- Default cognitive-layer foreground usefulness for Dream, Journey,
  Observatory, cognitive-load, Episode/Arc, or warm preactivation.

Use the
[`claim-boundary owner and retirement ledger`](current-claims.md#claim-boundary-owner-and-retirement-ledger)
to find what evidence would narrow or retire each material limit.
